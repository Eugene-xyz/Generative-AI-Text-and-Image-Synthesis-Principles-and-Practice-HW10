 4.        CL 4      th Round             -  WILD CARD APPLICATION

 Further to the decision of the CEV Board of Administration, wild cards may be granted for
 participation in the 4th Round of the CEV Champions League Volley.
 The CEV is committed to             ensuring that      this process      is run and governed          with a strategic,
 balanced, and forward            - looking mindset.       The   evaluation      process     considers both sporting
 merit    and the overall contribution a club can make to the sporting, commercial and
 promotional development of the competition.
 The CEV hereby invites interested clubs to submit their applications for a wild card to
 enter the competition.
 Each application must include comprehensive information addressing the following
 evaluation      criteria:
     1. Sporting Merit & Competitiveness

      •    Recent national and international results
      •    Domestic league and/or              national    cup standing
      •    Performance in previous CEV competitions (if applicable)
      •    Ambition and competitiveness for the upcoming season
      •    Team roster

      2. Venue & Infrastructure

      •    Venue name, city, and capacity
      •    Compliance with CEV requirements (e.g., flooring, lighting)
      •    VIP, media, and team facilities
      •    Historical attendance and fan engagement figures                         in CEV and national
           competition      s

 8.        KNOCK OUT                     C  OMPETITION                     METHOD               –    UPDATE

 Further to the approval of the CEV Board of Administration on 03                              March 2026,       please be
 informed that        in a “Home and Away” match, the winner                    shall be   the Participant       that    wins

 considered tied, and the Golden Set procedure shall be applied to                                        determine the
 eventual winner        .

    We would like to kindly inform all participants that, on 23 September 2026, the CEV Board of
    Administration approved the implementation of the rule tests endorsed by the FIVB Board of
    Administration in CEV competitions, including the CEV Champions League V                                olley 2027.

    The implementation would apply from the beginning of                         the  competition      , ensuring that the same
    rules are maintained throughout the competition and, where applicable, throughout the entire
    2026/27 season.
    For the sake of clarity, the complete list and details of the rules included in the 2026 FIVB Rule
    Test can be found under the following link:                 FIVB Board of Administration approves rule tests for
    2026 competitions         –   FIVB

    It should also be noted that the implementation of the tested rules is subject to the conditions
    established by the relevant FIVB              and CEV      regulations. In particular, where            the application of a
    rule requires technological support, it shall only apply where the required technology is available
    and operational, in accordance                with the FIVB Video Challenge Regulations 2026.                      Furthermore,
    the application of certain tested rules may be subject to the physical characteristics and layout
    of the competition venue and court and shall therefore only be implemented where the court
    layout allows for their proper application.               (i.e Coach to speak to the first referee)

    CEV considers that this approach will provide the necessary regulatory certainty for all
    stakeholders and, most importantly, ensure a consistent and uniform application of the Rules of
    the Game across European volleyball throughout the 2026/27 season.
